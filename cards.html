Welcome to the Route Cards

What are the route cards?

Our route cards are a fun and rewarding way to measure your progress in Strides! We have different rarities of cards featuring all sorts of distances and difficulties. You don’t have to run the entire route to collect its route card unless it’s of Legendary rarity. In order for a run to qualify for a card, it must be during our official runs (4pm Mon-Fri, 9am Sat) or organized in the unofficial-runs channel in our discord. Once you've collected a card, you can do whatever you want with it!

Looking at the cards on our website, you're able to see what the route card looks like, the art credits, the route's description, and a clickable map (takes you to the onthegomap site for the route for easier visual access)
