Welcome to the Route Cards

Rules for getting route cards:

  1. Must be completed during an official run, or organized with others in an unofficial run.
  2. Cards will be distributed to anyone who runs with Strides on a given day, regardless of how much of the full route they completed (with the exception of Legendary routes).
  3. Only one card will be given to each runner per run (ex. if somebody runs Farms & Swing on a single run, that day they will choose to be given either a Farms or Swing card, not both) and no duplicates.
  4. Also we are updating some of the cards this coming year, so if you want to switch out your old ones for the new ones feel free to ask us!
