Who are we?

We're a social, casual distance running club at UCSD for runners of all skill levels — from beginners who are just interested in giving it a try, to seasoned athletes training for big races! You'll almost always have someone to run with and you're guaranteed to make a few new friends. We also run in local races and host a variety of social events.

Where can you find us?

We meet every weekday (Monday thru Friday) at 4:00 PM, and Saturdays at 9:00 AM. We meet at the front of RIMAC gym. And never worry about being late because we never start on the dot.

What if I'm slow?

Don't worry about how fast or slow you are, there will always be someone who's running at the same speed as you, or an officer who will be running besides you so never feel the need to push yourself, or feel alone!

How can I get involved?

Just coming to the runs/events, and getting to know everyone is a good way to get involved, no membership required! Stay up-to-date through our various social media platforms. We also open officer applications every quarter, and we have a big-little program! You can find our social media platforms at the bottom of our website, or through our LinkTree
